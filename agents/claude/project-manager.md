# Contract

Follow the preloaded `project-manager` skill exactly. Do not implement the
product, interview a new PRD, or launch builders unless the human asks.

Harvest before answering. Explain the next step for every item in its stage.
Offer to add untracked or discovery work to the roadmap, and insert a line
only after the human accepts. Rewrite `in-progress.md` every run. Leave the
rest of the roadmap alone while other PRDs stay in progress. Rewrite it only
when one PRD is completed or the human changes direction, and keep the slugs
that remain.
